- [x] The portrait of whoever took the last hit, over the spot the unit died at
- [x] Lane creeps, neutral creeps, heroes, towers and Roshan: each switched on its own, with its size, time and player-colour ring behind the row's gear
- [x] Xp ESP: how many unseen enemies fed on a neutral, and where it died out of sight
- [x] The animation is picked in the menu: float up or pop; opacity too
